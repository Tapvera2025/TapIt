Revamped HR Recruitment Flow

Here's the full end-to-end pipeline we're building — confirm this is right before we design each stage

1

Job Post Creation

HR creates a job post (title, department, description, requirements, rounds). Gets a shareable form link + one-click redirect icons to LinkedIn, Naukri, Indeed, Internshala etc. to post manually.

↓
2

Resume Intake

Resumes arrive via the public form link OR HR manually uploads them. All land in the Resume Inbox tagged by source (form link / manual / platform).

↓
3

Initial Screening

HR reviews submissions. Clicking "Move to Interview" auto-fills a candidate form from the parsed resume (name, email, phone, skills, experience). HR adds screening notes.

↓
4

Custom Interview Pipeline

HR defines rounds per job post (e.g. HR Screening → Technical Round → Managerial → Final HR). Each round: set mode (online/offline), assign an interviewer from company employees. Candidate moves round by round — Pass → next round or Fail → rejected.

↓
5

Interviewer Panel

Assigned interviewer (e.g. Tech Lead) gets a notification. They see the candidate details, job context, and previous round notes. After the interview they submit: rating, pass/fail, feedback, strengths, areas for improvement.

↓
6

Offer & Joining Email

Once accepted, HR sends a customizable joining email to the candidate. The email contains an "Acknowledge Now" button (unique tracked link). When the candidate clicks it, HR sees the acknowledgement status in the CRM.

↓
7

Employee Import

When the candidate joins on the mentioned date, HR clicks "Import to Employee" — the employee addition form is pre-filled from all candidate data already in the system. HR reviews and confirms.

Does this flow look right? Let me know in the terminal if anything needs adjusting — then we'll design each stage in detail.
