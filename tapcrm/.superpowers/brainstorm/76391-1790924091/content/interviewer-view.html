When the Tech Lead is assigned to a candidate's round, where do they go and what do they see?

Inside the CRM — "My Interviews" tab

Interviewer gets a CRM notification. They click it → lands on a "My Interviews" tab under Recruitment showing only interviews assigned to them. They open a candidate, fill feedback, and submit.
