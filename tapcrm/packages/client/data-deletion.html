Delete your data

Last updated 14 November 2026

You can remove Tapvera CRM's access to your Google, YouTube, Facebook, Instagram or LinkedIn account at any time, and ask us to delete the data we hold about you. This page explains how.

1. Disconnect an account in Tapvera CRM

  1. Sign in to Tapvera CRM. Clients sign in at the client portal.
  2. Open Social accounts.
  3. Choose the account and click Disconnect.

We delete the account's sign-in straight away, and the data we copied from it within 7 days. Scheduled posts for that account are cancelled.

2. Remove our access on the platform

You can also remove Tapvera CRM directly in the platform's settings, even without signing in to Tapvera CRM:

  • Facebook: go to Settings and privacy → Settings → Business integrations, select Tapvera CRM and click Remove.
  • Instagram: Instagram accounts are connected through a Facebook Page, so remove Tapvera CRM in Facebook as above.
  • Google and YouTube: open the Google security settings page, select Tapvera CRM and choose to remove its access.
  • LinkedIn: go to Settings → Data privacy → Permitted services, and remove Tapvera CRM.

When you remove our access this way, we delete the data we copied from that account within 30 days.

3. Ask us to delete your data

To ask us to delete data we hold about you, including data from a connected account, email tapveratechnologies@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request" and include:

  • your name and the email address you use with Tapvera CRM;
  • the company or agency whose workspace you use, if any; and
  • the Facebook Page, Instagram account, YouTube channel, Google account or LinkedIn profile concerned, if your request is about a connected account.

We may ask you to confirm your identity. We will reply with a reference for your request, and delete the data within 30 days.

4. What is not deleted

  • Posts and videos already published on Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, LinkedIn or Google stay there. Deleting data in Tapvera CRM does not remove them. Delete them on the platform itself.
  • Records the law requires us to keep, such as payroll, attendance and audit records, are kept for the required period and then deleted. We will tell you what we kept and why.
  • Records your employer or agency added are controlled by that company. We will pass your request to them and help them act on it.

For more detail, read our Privacy Policy, especially section 4 on connected accounts.
