What Tapvera CRM does

Tapvera CRM is business software for service companies and agencies. Companies sign up for a workspace and add their own staff and clients. It is not a consumer app.

  • People and HR

    Attendance (including biometric attendance devices), day and night shifts, breaks, leave, holidays, expenses and payroll.

  • Clients and projects

    Client records, projects and tasks, plus a client portal where clients follow progress, send messages and join meetings.

  • Sales

    Leads, follow-ups, callbacks, proposals and deals, with clear guidance on what to do next for each lead.

  • Recruitment

    Job applications, candidate tracking and hiring email templates.

  • Team chat and meetings

    Team messages and files, and meetings booked on Google Calendar with a Google Meet link sent to every guest.

  • SEO and social media

    Search performance from Google Search Console, and posts scheduled to Facebook Pages, Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube and Google Business Profile, with the results in one report.

How connecting Google, YouTube and social accounts works

Agencies use Tapvera CRM to publish posts and videos for their clients. Nothing is posted without an account owner first giving permission on the platform's own sign-in page.

  1. Sign in on the platform The account owner clicks Connect and signs in on Google's or Facebook's own page. Tapvera CRM never sees their password.
  2. Choose what to allow The platform shows exactly what Tapvera CRM asks for. The owner picks which Pages, channels or business locations to connect.
  3. Publish and see results The team prepares posts and videos. Tapvera CRM publishes them at the scheduled time and shows views, followers and engagement in reports.
  4. Disconnect any time The owner can disconnect in Tapvera CRM, or remove access in their Google or Facebook settings.
