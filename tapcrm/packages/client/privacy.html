1. Who we are

Tapvera CRM is provided by Tapvera Technologies Private Limited, a company in India ("Tapvera", "we", "us"). This policy covers the Tapvera CRM web app at app.tapvera.io, its client portal, and these public pages.

2. Our role and your company's role

Most information in Tapvera CRM is added by the companies that use it, for example an employer adding its staff, or an agency adding its clients. For that information, the company decides what is collected and why. It is the "data fiduciary" under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and we process the information on its behalf and on its instructions.

For the information we need to run the service itself, such as sign-in details, security logs and the connections described in section 4, we decide how it is used, and this policy applies directly.

If you are an employee or client of a company that uses Tapvera CRM, please send requests about your records to that company first. If you write to us instead, we will pass your request to them and help them answer it.

3. Information we collect

Account and sign-in information

  • Your name, work email address, phone number, role and the company you belong to.
  • Your password, which we store only in a hashed (scrambled) form.
  • Sign-in sessions: the device and browser you use, IP address, and times you signed in.

Information your company adds

  • Staff records: attendance, shifts, breaks, leave, holidays, expenses, payroll and documents.
  • Client, lead and sales records, proposals, projects and tasks.
  • Job applications and candidate details.
  • Chat messages, notes, files and meeting details.

Location

If your company turns on location-based sign-in, we use your device's location when you sign in, only to decide whether to allow the sign-in and to record that decision. These location readings are deleted automatically after 90 days by default.

Biometric attendance

If your company uses a biometric attendance device, we receive only the attendance punches (your ID on the device and the time). Fingerprints and face data stay on the device and are never sent to us.

Technical information

Like most websites, our servers record basic technical details such as IP address, browser type, pages requested and errors, to keep the service secure and working.

4. Google, YouTube and other connected accounts

Tapvera CRM lets a company, or its client through the client portal, connect accounts on Google, YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and LinkedIn. You connect an account by signing in on the platform's own page and approving the permissions it shows you. We never see your password for those platforms.

4.1 What we access and why

Service What we access Why we need it
Google account basics Your Google account ID and email address. To know which Google account was connected and show it to you.
YouTube Your channel name and ID, details of your videos, and channel and video statistics such as views, watch time, likes, comments, shares and subscribers gained or lost. Permission to upload videos. To upload the videos your team schedules, with the title, description and visibility chosen, and to show how your channel and videos perform.
Google Business Profile The business locations you manage and their performance figures, such as views, website clicks, calls and direction requests. Permission to publish posts. To publish the posts your team schedules and show location reports.
Google Search Console Search performance for websites you have access to, such as clicks, impressions, average position and search queries. Read-only. To show SEO reports. We cannot change anything in Search Console.
Google Calendar Your free and busy times, and the calendar events that Tapvera CRM creates for you. To find a free time for a meeting, and to create, update or cancel it with a Google Meet link and invitations to guests.
Facebook Pages The Pages you manage, their follower counts, and reactions, comments and shares on posts. Permission to publish posts. To publish the posts your team schedules and show results.
Instagram Business or creator accounts linked to your Facebook Pages, their follower and post counts, and likes and comments on posts. Permission to publish posts. To publish the posts your team schedules and show results.
LinkedIn Your name and LinkedIn member ID, the company pages you manage, and post statistics where LinkedIn allows. Permission to publish posts. To publish the posts your team schedules and show results.

We only ask for the permissions a feature needs, and only when you connect that feature.

4.2 How we use this data

We use data from connected accounts only to provide the features you can see in Tapvera CRM:

  • publishing the posts and videos your team prepares, at the time you choose;
  • showing the accounts, channels, Pages and locations you connected; and
  • showing reports on how your posts, videos, websites and locations perform.

We do not:

  • sell this data;
  • use it for advertising, including personalised, retargeted or interest-based ads;
  • use it to check anyone's creditworthiness or for lending decisions;
  • use it to develop, improve or train general artificial intelligence or machine learning models; or
  • share it with anyone except as described in section 6.

Our staff do not read this data, except when you ask us to (for example, for support), when needed for security (for example, to investigate abuse or fix a bug), when the law requires it, or when it has been combined and made anonymous for our internal operations.

4.3 Google API Services User Data Policy

Tapvera CRM's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4.4 YouTube

Tapvera CRM uses YouTube API Services to upload videos and read channel and video information and statistics. By using the YouTube features of Tapvera CRM, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

4.5 How we store and delete this data

  • Sign-in tokens from Google, YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and LinkedIn are stored encrypted. We keep them only while the account stays connected.
  • Statistics (such as YouTube views and Business Profile performance) are kept so you can see reports over time, while the account stays connected. At least every 30 days we check that we still have permission, and refresh the data.
  • Other copied data, such as channel names and video details, is refreshed or deleted at least every 30 days.
  • When you disconnect an account in Tapvera CRM, we delete its sign-in straight away and delete the data we copied from it within 7 days. For Google and YouTube, disconnecting also ends Tapvera CRM's access with Google. For Facebook, Instagram and LinkedIn, you can also choose to remove our access on the platform.
  • When you remove access on the platform instead (see 4.6), we delete the data we copied from that account within 30 days.
  • Deleting data in Tapvera CRM does not delete anything already published on YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn or Google. To remove a post or video, delete it on that platform.

4.6 Removing our access

You can disconnect any account in Tapvera CRM. You can also remove our access directly on each platform at any time:

  • Google and YouTube: the Google security settings page (Third-party apps with account access).
  • Facebook and Instagram: in Facebook, go to Settings and privacy → Settings → Business integrations, and remove Tapvera CRM.
  • LinkedIn: Settings → Data privacy → Permitted services.

Our data deletion page explains every way to remove your data.

5. How we use information

  • To provide Tapvera CRM and the features your company has turned on.
  • To keep accounts secure, prevent fraud and abuse, and keep audit records.
  • To send service emails, such as invitations, password resets and alerts.
  • To provide support when you contact us.
  • To meet our legal obligations.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

6. Who we share information with

  • The company that runs your workspace, such as your employer or agency, according to its settings.
  • The platforms you connect. When we publish a post or video for you, its content is sent to that platform.
  • Service providers who help us run Tapvera CRM, such as hosting and file storage, network security (Cloudflare), email delivery, and Google for calendar and meeting features. They may use the data only to provide their service to us.
  • Authorities, when Indian law or a valid legal order requires it.
  • A buyer or successor, if our business is merged or sold. We will tell you before your information becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

7. Cookies and browser storage

We keep you signed in using your browser's session storage, which is cleared when you close the tab. While you connect a platform account, we set a short-lived secure cookie that protects the connection from forgery. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. Our pages load the Inter font from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google's servers to download it.

8. How long we keep information

  • Account information: while your account is active.
  • Information your company adds: for as long as your company keeps it, according to its settings.
  • Location readings from location-based sign-in: 90 days by default.
  • Notifications: 1 year.
  • Payroll, attendance and audit records: up to 7 years, because Indian law requires these records to be kept.
  • Data from connected accounts: as described in section 4.5.
  • When a company closes its workspace, we delete its data within 90 days, except records we must keep by law.

9. How we protect information

  • All traffic to Tapvera CRM is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Sign-in tokens for connected platforms are encrypted when stored.
  • Passwords are stored only in hashed form.
  • Each company's data is kept separate, and access is controlled by roles.
  • Sensitive actions are recorded in an audit trail.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal data, we will tell the affected company and people, and the Data Protection Board of India, as the law requires.

10. Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can:

  • ask for a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how we use it;
  • ask us to correct, complete or update it;
  • ask us to delete it, where we are not required by law to keep it;
  • withdraw consent you gave, for example by disconnecting a connected account;
  • nominate someone to use these rights for you if you die or cannot act; and
  • make a complaint to us, and if you are not satisfied, to the Data Protection Board of India.

To use these rights, write to tapveratechnologies@gmail.com. We may ask you to confirm your identity first. For records your company added, see section 2.

11. Children

Tapvera CRM is for businesses and is not meant for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data about children. If you think we have, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. If the change is important, we will also tell company administrators by email or in the app before it takes effect.
